DUCT AREA NETS - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Gottigere Duct Area Nets: Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers about Sarathi Duct Area Nets, suitable duct spaces, installation methods, customization, materials and service availability in Gottigere and Bengaluru.

What are duct area nets used for?

+

Duct area nets are used to cover suitable open shafts, utility ducts, service openings and similar spaces. Depending on the site, they can help reduce bird entry and the movement of lightweight debris through exposed duct areas.

Can Sarathi Duct Area Nets be installed in apartment ducts?

+

Yes. Sarathi Duct Area Nets can be customized for suitable apartment shafts and utility openings. The installation is planned after checking the duct dimensions, fixing surfaces, pipes, cables and other obstructions within the space.

Can duct nets be fitted around pipes and service lines?

+

Yes. Duct spaces often contain pipes, cables, exhaust outlets and other service components. Sarathi Duct Area Nets are measured according to the actual opening so the netting can be planned around existing obstructions while maintaining necessary service access.

Will a duct area net stop pigeons from entering the shaft?

+

A properly planned duct net can help close suitable bird-entry routes through an exposed shaft. However, the entire opening, perimeter and service gaps should be inspected because small unprotected openings can still provide access for birds.

Are duct area nets customized according to the opening?

+

Yes. Ducts can differ considerably in width, depth, height and shape. Sarathi Duct Area Nets are planned according to the dimensions and structure of the particular site, including available edges and fixing points.

Does the duct need to be inspected before installation?

+

A site assessment is useful before installation. The team can check the duct size, access points, pipes, exhausts, fixing surfaces and maintenance requirements. This helps determine how the duct protection net should be fitted for the particular property.

Is Sarathi Duct Area Nets available for installation in Gottigere?

+

Yes. Sarathi Duct Area Nets covers Gottigere and other parts of Bengaluru with customized duct net measurement and installation. Reach out to the team to discuss your duct layout and installation needs.
